Getting Started

From zero, and the principles to align on first.

Where should we start from zero?expand_more
Canonical datasets + a few dozen offline evals + one thin knowledge skill capture most of the upside. You don't need the whole stack to launch.
How much does current accuracy matter vs. future improvement?expand_more
Decide up front. It changes how much you invest in validation before launch versus iterating in production.
How much business complexity should we expect to grow into?expand_more
Anticipated complexity drives how much you invest in the semantic layer and business context graph now.
How technical is the intended audience?expand_more
A more technical audience tolerates raw fields and caveats; a business audience needs governed, unambiguous answers.
What's our budget for accuracy?expand_more
Adversarial validation buys about +6% accuracy but costs +32% tokens and +72% latency. Decide whether that trade is worth it per domain.
How comfortable are we with data access controls?expand_more
Your comfort with access controls sets the boundary of what the agent may read — and shapes the safeguards you need.
